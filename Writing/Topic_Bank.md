# Topic Bank

## Education

### Question
Should universities require students to take arts courses?

### My position
Yes. A broad education is useful beyond students' majors.

### Idea chain
Different courses → wider perspectives → better collaboration

### Useful chunks
- a broader curriculum
- expose students to diverse perspectives
- look beyond one's major
- develop transferable skills
- collaborate with people from different backgrounds
- prepare students for an interdisciplinary workplace

### My example
A computer-science student can learn basic design principles and create more user-friendly products.
